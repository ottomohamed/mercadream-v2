Questions

Do I need any music or editing skills?

No. You write — or co-write with AI — the lyrics. MercaDream Studio composes and sings the song for you.

How do credits work?

You buy GNS credits once (1 GNS = $0.10) and spend them when you create. There is no subscription. A full song costs 5 GNS.

What happens if a generation fails?

If our provider rejects your request before creating anything, your credits are returned automatically.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
