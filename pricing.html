Questions

When are my credits added?

Right after Stripe confirms your payment — usually within a few seconds. Your balance updates on its own; you don't need to refresh.

Do credits expire?

No subscription is needed. Your credits stay in your account until you use them.

What happens if a generation fails?

Credits are used when production starts. If the music engine rejects your request before creating anything, your credits are returned automatically.

Can I get a refund for unused credits?

Credit purchases are final once credits are added to your account. If you were charged but no credits arrived, contact us and we will fix it.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe, a certified payment provider. MercaDream Studio never sees or stores your card details.
