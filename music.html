How to get an ownership contract

  1. Lock the title. Click 🔒 Lock next to the title. The date and time are recorded.
  2. Write and lock the lyrics. Write them yourself or with the AI songwriter, edit as you like, then click 🔒 Lock lyrics. We measure the share you wrote yourself.
  3. Produce the song. The genre, mood, voice, tempo and music engine are recorded.
  4. Issue the contract — 10 GNS. Enter the holder's full legal name: a person or a company. You can add your artist or channel name.

What the contract contains

  • An authorship record: what you created and what the AI generated.
  • MercaDream Studio's assignment to you of any rights it may hold in the song, and a waiver of royalties.
  • The digital fingerprint of the audio file and the lyrics.
  • A unique contract number and QR code that anyone can check on our verify page.

Good to know

  • A contract is available only for songs whose title and lyrics were locked before production.
  • The holder should be a real person or a company — a channel is not a legal owner. Put your channel name in the "artist or channel name" field.
  • The holder's name cannot be changed after the contract is issued.
  • A contract song is produced in one version.
